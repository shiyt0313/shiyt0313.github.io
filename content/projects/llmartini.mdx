## Overview

LLMartini helps users compare and combine responses from multiple language models in a shared interactive workspace. It aligns related content, highlights differences, and supports user-controlled composition to bring together the complementary strengths of different models.

## Core Idea

The system decomposes responses into semantically aligned segments, automatically merges consensus content, and highlights
model differences while preserving unique contributions.
