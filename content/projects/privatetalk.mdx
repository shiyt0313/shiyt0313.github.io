## Overview

PrivateTalk activates voice input through a natural hand-on-mouth gesture, detected by comparing audio signals from two Bluetooth earphones. The gesture reduces speech exposure and conceals lip movements while enabling convenient voice interaction without wake words or button presses.

## Why it matters

The gesture reduces the spread of voice, conceals lip movement from bystanders, and improves privacy in public settings.
