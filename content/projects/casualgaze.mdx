## Overview

CasualGaze enables object selection through natural, casual glances rather than sustained precise fixation. It models the spatial and temporal characteristics of gaze behavior to infer intended targets and support efficient, comfortable gaze-based interaction.

## Findings

The usability evaluation showed substantial improvements in recognition and selection speed compared with baseline
techniques.
