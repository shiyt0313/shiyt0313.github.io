## Overview

HeadCross enables hands-free target selection in augmented and virtual reality through deliberate head movements across target boundaries. Its recognition method distinguishes intentional selection gestures from ordinary head motion, supporting interaction when users’ hands are occupied.

## Why it matters

The technique supports hands-free selection, which is useful when hands are occupied by other tasks.
