## Overview

CalibRead calibrates eye tracking through natural reading by modeling the relationship between gaze patterns and text structure. It embeds calibration into an everyday activity, reducing setup effort and interruptions.

## Findings

The method improves calibration accuracy for focused reading and skimming, outperforming a traditional seven-point
calibration baseline in the reported evaluation.
