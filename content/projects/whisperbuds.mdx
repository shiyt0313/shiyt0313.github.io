## Overview

WhisperBuds enables real-time whispered speech input using commercial earbuds, allowing users to enter text quietly in shared spaces. It combines multichannel neural noise cancellation with a fine-tuned speech recognition model to deliver fast, accurate transcription while minimizing disturbance to others.

## Contribution

WhisperBuds explores how commodity earbuds can support low-error whisper input and more discreet voice interaction in
everyday settings.

## Why it matters

This project fits the broader research agenda around wearable sensing, practical interaction design, and more socially
acceptable AI-enabled interfaces.
