## Overview

COMETIC improves smartphone eye tracking by collecting implicit calibration data during cursor-based interaction. It identifies reliable cursor positions as gaze proxies and updates the eye-tracking model during use, reducing the need for dedicated calibration tasks.

## Core Idea

The system uses cursor-based interaction and the correlation between cursor motion and eye movement to enable implicit
calibration through natural interaction.
