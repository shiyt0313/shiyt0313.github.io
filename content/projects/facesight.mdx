## Overview

FaceSight enables hand-to-face gesture interaction on AR glasses using a compact, downward-facing infrared camera. By recognizing hand–face contact, fingertip movements, and nose deformation, it supports rich gesture input and continuous control around the user’s face.

## Contribution

The approach expands sensing capability on AR glasses for hand-to-face gesture interaction.
