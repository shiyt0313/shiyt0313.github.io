## Overview

ConeSpeech enables users to direct their speech toward selected listeners in shared virtual environments. Adjustable, cone-shaped delivery areas support flexible conversations with individuals or groups while reducing interruptions and overhearing by nearby participants.

## Core Idea

Users look toward the target listener, and only listeners inside the cone-shaped region in that direction can hear the
speech.
