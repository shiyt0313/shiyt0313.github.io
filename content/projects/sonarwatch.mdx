## Overview

SonarWatch combines ultrasound and motion sensing to recognize gestures and nearby actions around a smartwatch. By using sensors available on the device, it expands wearable interaction and behavior sensing beyond the small touchscreen.

## Contribution

The project combines IMU and acoustic sensing to support same-side and opposite-side hand interactions with attention to
power efficiency.
